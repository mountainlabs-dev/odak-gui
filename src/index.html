What matters to you

Choose up to two. Odak's Air Coach watches the triggers behind them more closely and suggests actions on its screen. Insights shows how your air changed for each of them.

Your day

Tell Odak when you wake up, focus, rest and sleep. Each part starts at its time and lasts until the next one, so sleep can run past midnight. Odak rates the air for each part of your day on its My day screens, and the Insights tab uses the same schedule.
